# Genre research

## Visual cue

The UI world matches the observed Webild luxury-real-estate template ([luxury-real-estate](https://www.webild.io/templates/luxury-real-estate)): full-bleed hero with a left espresso overlay and paired pill CTAs; centered about band with one wide rounded plate; products as a two-column listing grid (image above, short caption below); contact as a dark photograph plus a translucent warm-brown form; ivory multi-column footer. That template is a **visual cue**, not a sector cue. Copy, IA, proof, and buyer path stay industrial B2B — scaffolding and formwork procurement from Chakan. Villa/listing language is translated to product collections. No invented lifestyle claims; photographs are atmospheric, not the works.

Category research below is unchanged: manufacturer-site patterns for trust, RFQ, and export proof.

## Category research

Common success traits from scaffolding and formwork manufacturer sites (Layher, PERI, Doka, and the same class of exporters: Altrad, ULMA, BrandSafway-type industrial suppliers). Only recurring patterns were applied. No marks, product-system names, load tables, or certifications were copied.

## Recurring patterns observed

1. **Thirty-second homepage verification.** Category leaders state what they make (scaffolding / formwork systems), who they serve (contractors, industry, events), and why delivery is believable (plant, quality system, network) above the fold. Applied: home first viewport names scaffolding and formwork, Chakan since 2014, process register, and a quote/call pair.

2. **Trust signals early, and only real ones.** Layher leads with “Made in Germany” and years in trade; PERI and Doka lead with engineering support and footprint. Applied: manufacturing since 2014, zero-PPM commitment already published by ProNext, named export regions, and the process list. No invented ISO numbers or client logos.

3. **Products and capabilities within two clicks.** Every leader exposes a Products (or Systems) path from the first screen; process and technical evidence are not buried in “About.” Applied: Products and Manufacturing in primary nav; home links to both; process list is on the home register and expanded on `/manufacturing`.

4. **Export / region proof.** Global houses publish country networks. Applied: the six regions ProNext already names (Germany, Mauritius, Latin America, Philippines, Dubai, Pakistan), shown early and again on Quality and Contact — as a footprint statement, not a fake office map.

5. **Phone and RFQ always available.** Category sites keep a contact/enquiry control in the header and repeat it in the footer. Applied: header quote + both numbers; footer repeats email and phones; sticky mobile call bar to `+91 9422513045`.

6. **Short RFQ.** Enquiry forms on these sites ask for identity and the job, not a long qualification survey. Applied: name, company, email, phone, need. Mailto default; Formspree via `NEXT_PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ID`.

7. **Mobile-first.** Construction buyers often call from site. Applied: stacked first viewport, large tap targets, sticky call, no hover-only information.

8. **Proof over adjectives.** Leaders show systems, drawings, plants, and documented ratings. ProNext has no public rating tables or plant photography. Applied: the process sequence, coating and CMM language they publish, hours, address, and persona reviews labeled as such — not “world-class solutions.”

9. **FAQ + SEO structure.** PERI-class sites answer rental vs purchase, lead time, and documentation in FAQ-like blocks and keep crawlable product/contact URLs. Applied: `/faq` with JSON-LD FAQPage; unique titles/descriptions; Organization schema; sitemap and robots.

10. **Direct technical contact path.** A named plant email and phone beats a ticket portal. Applied: `sumit.b@pronextindustries.com` and both mobiles on every page, plus an invitation to visit the Kharabwadi works.

## Patterns deliberately not copied

- Proprietary system families, load ratings, and rental fleets (not ProNext facts).
- Certificate badge walls and ISO numbers (incumbent heading has no listed numbers).
- Named customer logo rails (unverifiable).
- Stock “our team smiling in hard hats” photography as proof.
- Icon-card service grids and purple SaaS chrome (craft-floor bans + anti-reference to the incumbent template).

## How this site uses the traits

| Trait | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| 30-second verification | Home hero + editorial intro + collections |
| Real trust only | 2014, zero PPM, export list, process |
| Two-click depth | `/products`, `/manufacturing`, `/quality` |
| Export proof | Home rail, Quality, Contact |
| Always-on phone/RFQ | Header, footer, sticky call, `/contact` |
| Short RFQ | Five fields, mailto / Formspree |
| Mobile-first | Layout, tap targets, sticky call |
| Proof > adjectives | Process, coating, CMM, packing, hours |
| FAQ + SEO | `/faq`, metadata, JSON-LD, sitemap |
| Direct contact | Email, two phones, Chakan address |
