## Platform

## Stack

delegated: Next.js App Router + TypeScript with `output: "export"` so the site can ship as a Render free-plan static site (`out/`). Form handling is mailto by default, Formspree-ready via `NEXT_PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ID`.

## Users

Primary: procurement officers, project buyers, and contractor principals who need scaffolding and formwork components on a schedule. They arrive from a drawing, a packing list, or a missed lead time — not from brand browsing.

Secondary: site engineers and formwork planners who care about dimensional fit, coating, plywood fitment, and whether the plant can answer a drawing revision the same day.

Tertiary: overseas buyers and consolidators in the named export regions who need export packing, English drawings, and a reachable plant phone.

## Product Purpose

ProNext Industries Private Limited manufactures and markets scaffolding and formwork components from Chakan, Pune. The website exists so a first-time buyer can verify what the plant makes, how it is made, where it ships, and how to request a quote or call the works — within about thirty seconds.

Success is a qualified RFQ or a phone call, not a newsletter signup.

## Positioning

A Chakan plant that has been making scaffolding and formwork components since 2014, with a published zero-PPM quality commitment and a named export footprint (Germany, Mauritius, Latin America, Philippines, Dubai, Pakistan). The mechanism is in-house realization: design for the local market, laser and CNC forming, welding fixtures, CED plus powder coating, plywood cutting/fitment/sealant/riveting, and CMM inspection — one works, not a trading desk.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: process, hours, address, export list, and the zero-PPM claim already published — not “world-class” filler.
2. A buyer should reach products, process, and a quote path in two clicks or fewer.
3. Phone and RFQ stay available on every viewport, including a sticky mobile call control.
4. Never invent certifications, logos, or specifications to look more like a European system house.
5. Write like a plant talking to procurement: specific, calm, export-literate.
